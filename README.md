A one-page front page summing up **23 November 2026** in a single image:
the AI rout that hit Asia (Kospi −3.3%), Guterres' call to regulate AI
before it's too late, and the US lead over China at a record low — plus
10 more stories, 18 more in brief, a world map with numbered pins, and a
DATE CHECK box separating what happened on 23 November from what did not.

- 31 stories, 6 regions — every figure as reported on 23 Nov 2026.
- Scenes are drawn illustrations, not photographs.
- `draw.py` renders `entry.png` (1024×1024, PIL).
- `main.py` (stdlib only) copies `entry.png` to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` after validating the PNG structure.
